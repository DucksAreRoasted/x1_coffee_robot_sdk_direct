# MoveIt / PT investigation — 2026-10-27

Status: PVT jogging restored and confirmed by the operator. MoveIt real-hardware
execution through dynamic PT remains unresolved. Do not equate unit-test success,
100 Hz write callbacks, service acknowledgement, or an accepted action with
verified physical motion.

## Observations

- Startup at 19:12 armed PT. Both-arm execution reported left success; right
  failed measured arrival with errors -0.236160, -0.362779, -0.126321 rad.
  Operator reports neither arm moved and no emergency stop was pressed.
- At epoch 1793099557.795405426 eight drives reported alarm=1, enabled=0,
  follow_error=0: physical axes 6 through 13. This is later than the goal failure.
- A second startup at 19:15 reached mechanical zero without needing a PVT move,
  armed PT at 1793099729.056851397, and developed the same fault at
  1793099754.375177531 while the operator did nothing.
- Current PVT startup (PID 53000, started at epoch 1793099958) explicitly leaves
  PT unarmed. Checked through epoch 1793100372: about 409 seconds of 100 Hz
  operation, without read/write faults in that node log. Operator confirms
  translation works again. This supports investigating PT initialization/idle
  streaming; it does not distinguish coordinate reset from FIFO/planner issues.

## Source-supported findings and changes

- Default startup leaves arm_for_moveit=false. motion_enabled=true still permits
  mechanical-zero PVT and normal jogging, without unconditionally starting PT.
- PVT senders now include one-waypoint count: sequence, seconds, 1, 15 joints.
- Both default trajectory position tolerances and PVT arrival feedback are checked.
- Consecutive FIFO stalls were incorrectly reset on each earlier non-full axis;
  reset now occurs only after all 15 axes have space. Tests cover blocked axes
  9 and 15 and recovery between bursts.
- PT logs distinguish successful PtStart and successful whole-frame enqueue from
  skipped FIFO frames. Every approximately 100 frames, read-only GetPrfPos and
  GetPrfMode sample the axis with largest command/encoder pulse discrepancy.
  Optional query failures are logged and never stop streaming by themselves.
- PT initialization still performs AxisOff, SetPrfPos, SetEncPos,
  SynchAxisPos, AxisOn, PrfPt, prebuffer, and PtStart. None of these steps has
  been established as the root cause of the idle drive fault.
